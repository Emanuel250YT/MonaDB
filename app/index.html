3 easy steps

No accounts and no subscriptions: your wallet is your login, and the key that opens your files is derived from it.

  1. Connect and unlock

    Connect MetaMask, Rabby or any EVM wallet and sign one message. Your encryption key is made in this tab and never leaves it.

  2. Upload privately

    Each file is compressed, encrypted and split into shards that paid nodes keep. You approve it once.

  3. Share and recover

    Give another wallet access and send the link. They open it with their own key, from any device.

More benefits

  • Erasure coded

    Any k of the n shards rebuild your file, so it survives lost nodes.

  • Storage proofs

    Nodes answer random challenges on Monad to get paid. A missing shard costs them.

  • Escrow comes back

    What the nodes did not earn returns to you: unfilled copies, abandoned uploads, unused storage.

  • Exact quotes

    See the storage fee, the gas and the total in MON before you sign anything.

  • Up to 1 GiB a file

    Big files are split into blobs and shards, and every piece is checked on the way back.

  • Wallet-to-wallet sharing

    Seal the file key to another wallet, send the link and revoke access any time.
